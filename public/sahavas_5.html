The lawyer's layer

Trust is engineered, not promised

🏦 We never touch your money

Sahavas is a system of record, not a custodian. Payments go straight to the society's own bank via licensed UPI gateways.

🔐 You enter your own credentials

KYC and eSign happen on your device via UIDAI / DigiLocker. We never hold your Aadhaar number or card details.

📋 DPDP-aligned by design

Explicit consent, purpose limitation, and data-principal rights at signup. Legal-hold records disclosed up front.

⚖️ Evidence, not a verdict

Sahavas produces court-ready evidence packages. It is not a court and does not give legal advice.

Honest pricing — no surprises

The platform is free. You pay only for what you use.

Sahavas earns by facilitating services that cost real money — Aadhaar verification, eSign, background checks, legal packages. We charge a small markup on each. The society and residents always see exactly what they're paying and why.
